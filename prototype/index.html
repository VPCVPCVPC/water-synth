How to play

  1. Touch the water. Ripples spread; stones ring when they reach them. You don't pick notes — the water does. Where you touch changes what plays, and the same touch never plays quite the same twice. Leave it and it falls quiet.
  2. Drag to draw a trail of ripples. Every finger is its own touch.
  3. Hold a finger still to rest a hand on the water: it calms there. Slide to calm along the way.
  4. Camera (top bar): move in front of it and your movement makes ripples — the picture stays on this device.
  5. Tap a stone to lift it, then tap the water to set it down. Its sound is under Voice.
  6. Tape records the water's sound and loops it — layer up to four tracks, each with its own fader to fade it in and out. TAPE at the top of the pond picks a track and ● REC starts a take, without leaving the water (on a computer: 1–4, then R).
  7. Record piece saves everything you hear as a WAV.

Knobs: drag the dial up or down; double-tap to reset. Your settings stay in this browser. The feedback link sends your device type and a few settings along with your answers.

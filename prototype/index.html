How to play

  1. Touch the water. Ripples spread; stones ring when they reach them. You don't pick notes — the water does. Where you touch changes what plays, and the same touch never plays quite the same twice. Leave it and it falls quiet.
  2. Drag to draw a trail of ripples; keep circling to stir the water up. One finger plays at a time: two fingers on the water are for the tape (below).
  3. Camera (top bar): move in front of it and your movement makes ripples — the picture stays on this device.
  4. Tap a stone to lift it, then tap the water to set it down. Its sound is under Voice.
  5. Tape records the water's sound and loops it — layer up to four tracks, each with its own fader to fade it in and out. ● REC at the top of the pond primes the picked track: the take starts with your next touch of the water, and ends on the nearest beat when you press it again, so every tape is whole beats of the tempo. Tap TAPE for every track's keys and faders. Or, without leaving the water, swipe two fingers on the pond: up to rec (again to finish), down to play or stop, left / right to change track (on a computer: 1–4, R, P).
  6. Record piece saves everything you hear as a WAV.

Sliders: drag along one, or tap where you want it; double-tap to reset. Your settings stay in this browser. The feedback link sends your device type and a few settings along with your answers. Open this again any time from how to play at the top (? on a phone).
